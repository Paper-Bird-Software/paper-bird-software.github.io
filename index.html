Paper Doll Machine

Bake a modular 3D character into a layered 2D sprite library — one sheet per body part, every piece swappable, every angle covered.

Documentation

  • Modular equipment. One sprite sheet per part. Build armor sets and swap any piece without re-exporting.

  • Bake Style. Define the whole look in one asset — three-point lighting, rim light, self-shadow and material override.

  • Supersampling up to 8×. Premultiplied-alpha downscaling for clean edges with no dark halo.

  • Weapons and shadows. Equipment baked with the character, plus ground shadows on their own layer.

  • Isometric or top-down. Up to 16 directions, with a JSON manifest documenting every bake.

Studio

Fold it flat.

A paper bird is a flat sheet that becomes a figure — and then folds back down again. That is close to the work: taking something built in three dimensions and laying it out cleanly in two.

Paper Bird makes editor tooling for Unity, aimed at the slow, repetitive part of 2D production. The goal is simple — hours instead of weeks, without giving up control over how the art looks.
